THE BIG IDEA01 / AN INTRODUCTION

Not a straight line.
A conversation.

Jazz did not simply “influence” popular culture from a distance. Musicians, dancers, designers, filmmakers, and audiences carried its sounds and ideas into new spaces—sometimes in collaboration, sometimes amid unequal credit and control.

This exhibit follows a few of those routes. Listen for improvisation, syncopation, and a willingness to break the pattern. Look for the artists and communities who made those possibilities real.

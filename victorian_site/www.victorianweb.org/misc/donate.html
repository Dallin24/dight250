The Victorian Web relies on its readers for essential financial support. Please consider making a donation today -- your support is crucial to our mission. All gifts, no matter how large or small, are welcome.

*

Donations may be made via direct debit/ACH, credit card, Apple Pay, and Google Pay. If you would prefer to write a check, contact the Treasurer, Noah Landow, at [email protected] to make arrangements.

Founded in 1987 by George P. Landow at Brown University in Providence, Rhode Island, the Victorian Web is the internet's oldest and largest website devoted to Victoriana. The site contains more than one hundred thousand documents and attracts thousands of vistors per month. Primarily staffed by volunteers, the Victorian Web relies on donations to stay afloat. Your tax-deductible gift helps us to cover our operational expenses -- website hosting, site maintenance, and some editorial help.

You can access our online donation page here, or by clicking the "Donate" button at top right.

While the majority of our donations are one-time contributions, we sometimes hear from those who wish to contribute in a more enduring way. Remembering the Victorian Web in your will is a wonderful way to make a lasting contribution. If you are considering a bequest, please contact Noah Landow, Treasurer, at [email protected] for simple instructions. Learn more about making a bequest to the Victorian Web.

The Victorian Web Foundation is a registered 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. Contributions are tax-deductible to the extent permitted by law. Our tax identification number is 84-5014830.

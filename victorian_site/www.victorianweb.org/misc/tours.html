The Victorian Web ranges across the whole spectrum of British culture in the Victorian era. Uniquely, it aims to link all this material rather than present each webpage in isolation and offers a variety of tools for navigating the site successfully.

One of those tools is the virtual tour, which presents a series of pages that may be traversed simply by clicking "Next" at the bottom of each page. Choose a tour from the list below and enjoy your exploration!

  • Explore London through late Victorian photographs
  • Visit some Victorian parks
  • Explore the churches of A.W.N. Pugin (1812-1852), noted for his development of the Neo-Gothic style
  • Visit London's Clubland
  • Explore the Black Friar Pub, a classic Victorian pub in London
  • Enjoy the neoclassical architecture of Bath
  • Tour the Lake District or the Peak District
